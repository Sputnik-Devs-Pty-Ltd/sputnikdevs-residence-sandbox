# Multi-Month WIL Progression Guide (1-Month, 3-Month & 6-Month Placements)

Sputnik Devs Academy™ supports academic institutions requiring **1-Month (152 Hours)**, **3-Month (480 Hours)**, and **6-Month (960 Hours)** Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) placements without fragmenting mentorship or engineering quality.

All students progress through a unified **3-Stage Rotational Engineering Pipeline**:

---

## Stage 1: Core Specialization Track Mastery (Month 1 • Days 1–19 • 152 Hours)

* **Target Cohort:** All new WIL students (`1-Month`, `3-Month`, and `6-Month` programs).
* **Objective:** Master Git workflow, Codespaces, automated builds, daily standups, and the 19 daily milestones of your **Primary Specialization Track** (`Track 01 Backend`, `Track 02 Frontend`, `Track 03 Database`, `Track 04 Big Data`, or `Track 05 Applied AI`).
* **Verification:** Daily Gate 3 Coordinator Oral Defense (`Kenneth Katsande` or `Prince Nkiwane`) + Gate 4 Principal Engineer Sign-Off (`Takudzwa Mupanesure`).
* **Graduation Checkpoint:** **19-Day Intensive WIL Sprint (`152 Hours`)** students complete their final 30-minute Oral Defense on Day 19 and receive their Cryptographically Signed Certificate & Official Transcript.

---

## Stage 2: Cross-Track Rotation & Multi-Disciplinary Pod Capstone (Months 2–3 • 480 Hours Cumulative)

* **Target Cohort:** **3-Month Semester WIL (`480 Hours`)** and **6-Month Diploma/Degree WIL (`960 Hours`)** students who have passed Stage 1.
* **Month 2 — Secondary Track Cross-Rotation (160 Hours):**
  * Each student rotates into a complementary **Secondary Track** so they become T-shaped engineers:
    * *Backend (`Track 01`) students* rotate into **Frontend UI (`Track 02`)** or **Database Engineering (`Track 03`)**.
    * *Frontend (`Track 02`) students* rotate into **Backend API (`Track 01`)**.
    * *Database (`Track 03`) students* rotate into **Big Data & BI (`Track 04`)** or **Backend API (`Track 01`)**.
    * *Big Data (`Track 04`) & Applied AI (`Track 05`) students* cross-rotate between **Analytics, AI Automation, and SQL Data Engineering**.
* **Month 3 — Multi-Disciplinary Pod Capstone & Staging QA/UAT Sprint (168 Hours):**
  * Students within **Pod Alpha (Richfield)** and **Pod Bravo (IIE Rosebank)** form a cross-functional product squad (1 Backend + 1 Frontend + 1 Database + 1 Data/AI) to deliver an end-to-end capstone module on the Residence Sandbox + execute structured User Acceptance Testing (UAT) & bug reproduction reports on Sputnik Devs staging platforms.
* **Graduation Checkpoint:** **3-Month WIL Learnership (`480 Hours`)** students complete their Stage 2 Capstone Defense and receive their dual-track Certificate & Official Academic Transcript.

---

## Stage 3: Senior WIL Associate, Peer Teaching Assistant (TA) & R&D Sprint (Months 4–6 • 960 Hours Cumulative)

* **Target Cohort:** **6-Month Credit-Bearing WIL Internship (`960 Hours`)** students who have passed Stages 1 and 2.
* **Key Responsibilities:**
  1. **Peer Teaching Assistant (Gate 2 Code Reviewer):** Stage 3 students act as first-line peer code reviewers for incoming Stage 1 students in their Pod, testing PR branches and coaching junior peers on Git hygiene before escalating to the Pod Coordinator.
  2. **Tertiary Track Rotation & Production Shadowing:** Complete a 3rd specialization rotation and shadow supervised staging deployments, telemetry monitoring, and automated regression testing.
  3. **R&D Innovation Capstone:** Design, benchmark, and document an advanced automation, analytics, or full-stack feature presented at the end of Month 6.
* **Graduation & Talent Pool Checkpoint:** **6-Month WIL Internship (`960 Hours`)** graduates receive our highest-tier **Multi-Track Engineering Certificate & Comprehensive Academic Transcript** and priority fast-track consideration for paid Junior Developer contracts at Sputnik Devs Studio (Pty) Ltd.
